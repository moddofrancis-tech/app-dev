# My Favorite Series: The Seven Deadly Sins


## Overview
**The Seven Deadly Sins** (*Nanatsu no Taizai*) is a fantasy anime based on the manga by
Nakaba Suzuki. Princess Elizabeth searches the kingdom of Britannia for the legendary
knights known as the Seven Deadly Sins, hoping they can help her save the kingdom from
the corrupt Holy Knights.

## Why I Love It
- Fun, distinct characters with their own personalities and powers
- A mix of **action**, **humor**, and **adventure**
- A big world with kingdoms, magic, and ancient races
